## Lead story — markets whipsaw on tariffs and the AI sell-off

President Trump's new tariffs on 14 trading partners landed on top of a
deepening AI sell-off, and US stocks swung violently on Wednesday. The
Nasdaq Composite fell as much as 4.6% at the open and closed down 0.62%;
the Nasdaq 100 briefly dropped 20% from its early-October peak — bear
market territory — before recovering. The S&P 500 closed down 0.25%,
while the Dow recovered from 1,100 points down at the open to close up
165 points (+0.33%). Asian markets had already slumped: South Korea's
KOSPI fell 6.16% and Japan's Nikkei 2.78%. Nvidia fell 3.9% on the day.
The tariffs follow an eight-month Section 301 probe and carry maximum
rates of 20–40% for the 14 partners named. US markets are closed on
Thursday 26 November for Thanksgiving.

## Grid stories

### Middle East — Houthis claim a strike on Saudi Arabia's biggest refinery

Yemen's Houthis said they attacked the Ras Tanura refinery, Saudi
Arabia's largest, and their spokesman said the attack caused fires. The
Saudi-led coalition said it shot down four Houthi drones over Ras
Tanura and three more in the Eastern Province. The claim widens the
energy front of the Iran war, a day after a Houthi strike on a Riyadh
tower injured 11 people.

### Space — Hera shows an asteroid flattened by a giant crater

The European Space Agency's Hera mission released its first images of
Dimorphos, the asteroid NASA's DART spacecraft struck in 2022. One face
of the 151 m moonlet is flattened into a crater nearly as wide as the
asteroid itself, the moonlet is tumbling chaotically, and hundreds of
boulders orbit the pair. DART hit Dimorphos on 26 September 2022 at
about 22,530 kph; Hera is observing from 25–30 km away and closes to
about 13 km in December.

### Economy — US prices up 3.8%, growth revised to 4.3%

Fresh US data showed an economy running hot: October PCE inflation was
3.8% over the year (core 3.3%), third-quarter GDP was revised up from
3.4% to 4.3% annualised, and jobless claims fell to 189,000, a
four-month low. Traders priced a roughly 95–97% chance of a
quarter-point Fed rate hike in December (the two sources quoted differ
slightly, so the poster states the range, not a single figure).

### Washington — senator demands answers on Huang's return

Top Senate antitrust Democrat Cory Booker demanded answers from Nvidia
about chief executive Jensen Huang's reinstatement under pressure from
President Trump. Huang resigned on 12 November and was reinstated on
Monday 24 November; the shares jumped more than 31% that Monday, then
fell about 7% on Tuesday and 3.9% on Wednesday. Huang faces a closed
House intelligence session on 1 December and a public Senate banking
hearing on 3 December.

### Climate — COP31 blame game

A day after the COP31 climate talks collapsed in Antalya without any
deal, OPEC's secretary general Haitham Al Ghais blamed the European
Union for the failure, while Pope Leo XIV expressed disappointment and
urged firm action to protect the Earth. The talks collapsed on Sunday
22 November — the longest COP ever and the first major breakdown since
Copenhagen 2009 — after the EU and about 40 countries rejected a final
text with no mention of moving away from fossil fuels. The poster dates
the collapse to 22 November and reports only the 25 November reactions
as the day's news.
